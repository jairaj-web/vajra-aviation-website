What is the Hybrid CPL Course?

The Hybrid CPL Course is a structured commercial pilot training pathway that combines classroom preparation and flying in one plan. You first complete 6 months (750 instructional hours) of DGCA CPL ground training at Vajra Aviation in Bangalore, taught by Ex-Indian Air Force officers. You then move to Flyola Aviation Academy for approximately 12 months of flying training, with around 200 planned flight hours.

Building the academic foundation first means you start flying with the theory already in place. Each phase has a clear training focus and a dedicated training partner: Vajra Aviation for ground instruction and Flyola Aviation Academy for flight training.

Two Training Phases

From aviation theory to the flight deck.

Phase 01 · Bangalore

Ground Training at Vajra Aviation

6 months · 750 instructional hours
  • All 7 DGCA CPL subjects: Air Navigation, Meteorology, Air Regulations, Technical General, Technical Specific, Flight Planning & Fuel Management, and Radio Telephony
  • DGCA examination preparation with mock tests and structured revision
  • Radio Telephony examination preparation
  • Taught by Ex-IAF faculty in small batches
Phase 02 · Flyola Academy

Flying Training at Flyola Aviation Academy

Approximately 12 months · 200 planned flying hours
  • Aircraft handling and foundational flight skills
  • Dual and supervised solo flight training
  • Cross-country navigation exercises
  • Instrument and night-flying components as applicable
  • Skill-test preparation aligned to the approved syllabus

Your 18-Month Roadmap

An indicative training plan, subject to individual performance and operational conditions.

01 · Months 1–6
Build your knowledge
Complete the 750-hour ground program at Vajra Aviation and prepare for the DGCA CPL theory examinations.
02 · Months 7–18
Take to the skies
Undertake approximately 200 hours of flying training at Flyola Aviation Academy, following its approved training syllabus.
03 · Licensing
Work toward your CPL
Complete the applicable DGCA examinations, medical, flight experience, skill tests and other licensing requirements.

Eligibility Criteria

  • 10+2 (HSC) with Physics and Mathematics
  • Minimum age 17 to start; 18 for issue of the CPL
  • DGCA Class 2 medical to start; Class 1 medical required for issue of the CPL
  • No prior aviation experience required
  • Proficiency in English
  • Final eligibility for each phase is subject to DGCA requirements and the training organisation’s admission criteria

Why Choose the Hybrid Pathway?

Focused instruction, practical progression. The hybrid course gives you a dedicated ground-training phase with Ex-IAF faculty in Bangalore, clear duration and instructional-hour targets for each stage, and a planned move into flying training with a specialist flight training organisation.

  • Dedicated ground-training phase: structured academic preparation before the flying phase
  • Defined training milestones: planned duration and instructional-hour targets for each stage
  • Two specialist training organisations: Vajra Aviation for ground instruction, Flyola Aviation Academy for flight training
  • Experienced faculty: Ex-IAF instructors and an ISO 9001:2015 certified institute
Please note: Training content, hour allocations, schedules and licensing eligibility are subject to the applicable DGCA requirements and approvals, weather, aircraft availability and individual progress. The two phases may require separate enrolment agreements. No admission, seat, price or licence outcome is guaranteed by submitting an enquiry.

Common Questions

Hybrid CPL FAQs

What is a hybrid CPL course?

A hybrid CPL course combines two phases in one planned pathway: classroom ground training for the DGCA CPL theory exams at Vajra Aviation in Bangalore, followed by practical flying training at Flyola Aviation Academy. You prepare for the written exams first, then build your flying hours.

How long does the hybrid commercial pilot training program take?

The pathway includes 6 months of ground training and approximately 12 months of flying training, or about 18 months in total. Actual completion may vary with individual progress, weather and aircraft availability.

How many hours of ground training are included?

The Vajra Aviation ground-training phase includes 750 instructional hours over 6 months, covering all 7 DGCA CPL subjects.

Where is the flying training conducted?

Flying training is planned at Flyola Aviation Academy. Please confirm the current operating base and available training slots with our admissions team before enrolment.

Does completing 200 flying hours guarantee a CPL?

No. A Commercial Pilot Licence is issued only after meeting all applicable DGCA requirements, including written examinations, medical standards, qualifying flight experience, skill tests and documentation.

Can I join only the ground training?

Yes. If you only need DGCA theory preparation, you can join our CPL Ground School on its own and complete your flying at any DGCA-approved flying school.

What are the fees and admission requirements?

Contact Vajra Aviation for the latest fee breakdown, entry requirements, payment milestones and training agreements for both phases. Call 6364919234 or 8111086614.
